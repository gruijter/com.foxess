Bring your Fox ESS solar inverter, home battery and energy meter into Homey through FoxCloud. Follow solar production, battery charge and grid import and export, see the inverter's own status and alarms, and use it all in Flows. FoxCloud updates every five minutes.

Homey can also steer the battery: choose its work mode, charge or discharge it at a set power, and change its minimum charge and the export limit. To connect, create an API key in FoxCloud under User Profile > API Management.
